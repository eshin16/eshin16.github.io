At Lune Coffee, every cup is made for anyone who enjoys great coffee, whether it is part of your daily routine or something you love to explore.

We source and import high quality beans to create coffee for every taste, while offering a variety of teas and seasonal drinks for non-coffee or non-caffiene drinkers.

Beyond beverages, you'll find sweet desserts and fresh breakfast favorites, including pastries, toasts, and sandwiches.

We value our customers and community here at Lune Coffee, and want to provide a welcoming space to slow down, connect, and enjoy something made well.
